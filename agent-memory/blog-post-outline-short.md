## The Hook

We built a native desktop app overnight — 40 commits, 200+ tests, lint
clean — while the human slept. But the interesting part isn't the output.
It's what this means for organizations that depend on expensive SaaS
and licensed software they don't control.

## The Problem (Three Problems, Actually)

1. **AI agents are powerful but chaotic.** Without structure, they drift,
   contradict themselves, and produce code that looks right but doesn't
   connect. Everyone's seen this.

2. **Organizations are captive to their vendors.** Enterprise SaaS is
   expensive, rigid, and evolves on the vendor's roadmap, not yours.
   Custom internal builds are the alternative — but they're slow, risky,
   and talent-constrained. So teams stay captive.

3. **Vibe coding has a day-two problem.** Business users and developers
   are vibe-coding real applications — and some of them are good. But IT
   won't touch them. No tests, no structure, no maintenance path. The
   app works today; who fixes it Tuesday? Who adds the feature Finance
   needs in Q3? The ops team sees a support burden with no process
   behind it, and they're right to push back.

## The Shift

What if building custom software was 10x faster and carried 10x less risk?

That's not a hypothetical. A structured AI agent process — where the
human investment is in *defining the problem correctly* and AI handles
*disciplined execution* — collapses the cost/risk equation that keeps
organizations locked into off-the-shelf software.

## The Process (Brief)

A reusable pipeline, not a one-off trick:

1. **Ideation** — conversational problem exploration
2. **PRD** — formal requirements with testable acceptance criteria
3. **TDD** — architecture and interfaces
4. **Issue decomposition** — right-sized work for autonomous agents
5. **Execution** — agents build in dependency order, checkpointing as they go
6. **QA** — layered verification (BUILD → BOOT → RENDER → FUNCTION → POLISH)
7. **Stabilize** — bug triage, fix, harden through the same pipeline
8. **Evolve** — new features, refactoring, and process tuning — full lifecycle

GitHub-native. Tech-stack agnostic. No SDK or framework — four markdown
files and discipline.

## Why This Is Transformative

- **Replace SaaS you don't control.** Build exactly what you need, on your
  infrastructure, evolving on your roadmap. The process makes this
  economically viable where it wasn't before.

- **Internal business transformation.** That system you've been wanting to
  build — the one that would change how your team operates but never
  survives the business case because "it'll take 6 months and 3 devs" —
  now survives the business case.

- **Maintain and evolve what you build.** This isn't throwaway prototyping.
  The same pipeline that builds the software handles bugs, features, and
  refactoring. It's a full lifecycle, not a demo.

- **Make vibe-coded apps enterprise-supportable.** Someone in your org
  already built the thing. The process wraps it: generates a PRD from
  what exists, reverse-engineers a TDD, adds tests, and — critically —
  gives the ops team the same issue-driven pipeline for bugs and feature
  requests going forward. IT says yes because they're not adopting an
  orphan; they're adopting a maintained system with a maintenance process.

- **Smaller teams, bigger ambition.** A solo developer or small team with
  this process can deliver what previously required a department.
